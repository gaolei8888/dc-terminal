## Shift+F3

- Cause (from code, not reproduced live): crossterm 0.28.1 treats `CSI 1;2 R` as a cursor-position reply and drops it, and xterm-style terminals send Shift+F3 as `ESC[1;2R`. `F8` is the reliable previous-session key; `Ctrl+F3` is grabbed by macOS.
- Fix: `fix-shift-f3` vendors crossterm with a patch for exactly `ESC[1;<2..8>R`, plus a pty byte-level test. Installed, **not confirmed on the user's terminal.** The user's `~/.tmux.conf` has `xterm-keys on`, `extended-keys off`. The session never saw the result of the user's `cat -v` check.
- Risks: vendored crate gets no upstream fixes; a future cursor query would misread row 1, columns 2-8 as a key.
